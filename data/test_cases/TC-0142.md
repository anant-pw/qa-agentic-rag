# TC-0142: Account lockout and cooldown after failed login attempts

**Module:** Login

## Preconditions
A registered user account exists with a known correct password. User is logged out.

## Steps
1. Navigate to /login
2. Enter the correct username with an incorrect password
3. Repeat step 2 two more times (3 total failed attempts)
4. Immediately attempt to log in with the CORRECT password
5. Wait for the defined cooldown period (5 minutes) to elapse
6. Attempt login again with the correct password

## Expected Result
After 3 failed attempts, the account is locked and step 4 must fail regardless of correct credentials, returning a clear lockout message (not a generic auth error). After the 5-minute cooldown, step 6 must succeed with the correct password.
