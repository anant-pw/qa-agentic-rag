# TC-0302: Exact SKU match ranks first in search results

**Module:** Search

## Preconditions
A product with a known unique SKU exists in the catalog, along with other products with similar text descriptions.

## Steps
1. Search for the exact SKU string (e.g. 'SKU-88213')
2. Observe the ranking position of the exact match in the results list

## Expected Result
The exact SKU match must appear as the first result. Fuzzy or partial text matches on unrelated fields must not outrank an exact identifier match.
